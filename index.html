"Hi, I'm calling on behalf of a patient named Lisa Tweardy with Stage IV pancreatic cancer who has a confirmed KRAS G12D mutation. Her oncologist, Dr. Alexandria Mara at Atrium Health Levine Cancer Institute, has already submitted expanded access paperwork for daraxonrasib, but we're concerned about the timeline. Then push for each of these:

Status of the pending request:

"The oncologist submitted a request under Dr. Alexandria Mara. Can you pull up whether there's a pending expanded access or compassionate use request under that name and tell me where it stands?"

Timeline:

"Given her prognosis, we can't wait weeks. What is the realistic timeline for a decision, and what would need to happen to shorten it?"

Case manager contact:

"I'd like the name and direct phone number of the case manager or medical affairs contact assigned to this file. I want to be able to follow up directly, not go through the main line each time."

Emergency / expedited review:

"This patient has a terminal diagnosis. Is there an expedited or emergency compassionate use pathway that applies here? If so, what does her doctor need to submit or resubmit to trigger that review?"

                  Tips for Every Call

                  • Call early morning (8-9am local time for whoever you're calling). Decision-makers are more reachable.
                  • Get a name and direct number from every person you speak with. Write it down.
                  • Use the word "terminal" -- it triggers different triage criteria than "serious illness."
                  • Ask "what's the single fastest path?" Sometimes enrollment in an open trial is faster than expanded access.
                  • Don't accept "check the website." Push for a human case manager every time.
                  • Log everything in the notes section of each call above -- date, time, name, what was said, next steps.
